08

Collaboration notes

Filippo drove the DigitalOcean web UI; Claude (chat) walked through every form field and verified before each Create button click. Peter was present but Filippo handled most clicks. Multiple times the UI auto-changed values when other fields were modified (region change reset machine type, switching CPU type reset plan tier) — every dropdown was re-verified after every other change to prevent surprises.

The session was longer than ideal (~3 hours) but produced more progress than expected. Two of twelve Phase 1 tasks complete, real infrastructure running, no regressions or rollbacks needed.

One process change came up mid-session: 2FA on the DigitalOcean account was initially declined, then enabled when its absence was flagged again before high-trust operations. The lesson: surface security questions early, and don't fight on them — re-raise them at the natural moment when their absence becomes load-bearing.
